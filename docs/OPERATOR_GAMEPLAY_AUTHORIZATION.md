# Standing Robotron operator authorization

Effective 2026-10-21, until explicitly revoked or modified by the operator.
Source: operator's “Charlie — Autonomous Robotron Marathon, Experience-to-Action
Learning, and Standing Gameplay Authorization” instructions in this session.
Project: AutoLearn ALA-2 / Charlie, repository mrgarmise/charlie, PR #9 integration.

The operator authorizes observation and autonomous Robotron play through the
existing fixed camera and established Xbox controller: START, movement, firing,
neutral/release, bounded gameplay experiments, authentic experience collection,
and evaluation of success, failure, score, survival and tactical consequences.
Charlie may run the existing Learning Executive, Reflection, Meditation, PPAL,
evaluation and memory mechanisms; autonomously choose eligible investigations;
generate hypotheses, investigate weaknesses and develop tactics; retain learned
knowledge across games, meditation, restarts and sessions; and use retained
learning to change perception, valuations, preferences, strategies and decisions.
Qualified evidence-backed gameplay adaptations may deploy through the existing
guarded integration and rollback mechanisms without renewed ordinary gameplay
permission. Prefer Charlie's retained findings; failures and inconclusive results
must inform later investigation rather than trigger blind repeated trials.

This is gameplay-specific authority, not technical qualification or unrestricted
hardware/external-system authority. Codex engineering changes and fixture results
must remain distinct from Charlie-originated discoveries and authentic learning.
Follow ALA_LEARNING_CHARTER.md and ALA2_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md; preserve original
ALA-2 obligations and independently establish E1, P1, I1, performance and autonomy.

Unauthorized without separate approval:

- Servo/head/camera repositioning, changing fixed focus or mechanical optics.
- Firmware deployment or wiring changes.
- Bypassing thermal, electrical, resource, watchdog or controller-release guards.
- Deploying unqualified models/policies, unbounded actuator exploration or unsafe commands.
- Uncontrolled repeated START during ambiguous states.
- Destructive changes to original observations, notebooks or historical evidence.
- Automatic merging into main, unrelated physical operations or external-system actions.

Use the strongest capability that passes applicable qualification and safety
requirements. Preserve the 75°C stop, throttling/resource stops, independent
monitoring, neutral release and physical emergency-stop authority. Missing any
required safety capability defers physical play. Cooling alone never authorizes
resumption of an interrupted trial. Do not repeat a failed attempt without
failure diagnosis and restored verified safety.

First trial is bounded to at most two complete games with a fresh evidence
root and frozen software, policy, configuration and learning-state identities.
Independently accept Game 1 completion and genuine playable Game 2 before
advancing to a three-game Block A, offline Reflection/Meditation with devices
released, and three-game Block B with retained learning. Retain phase reviews,
preflight and evidence acceptance; do not turn this into indefinite play.
Materially different physical risks or operating limits require separate approval.

Revocation: the operator may explicitly revoke or narrow this authorization in
any later instruction. On receipt, stop initiating affected actions, safely
neutralize/release active gameplay, preserve evidence, and record the effective
date and changed scope here. Do not erase this original grant or interpret
technical failure as a grant. This document supersedes earlier documentation's
requirement to ask again for ordinary gameplay within this standing scope;
all independent qualification and safety gates remain mandatory.
