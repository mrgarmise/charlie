## Trust boundary that must exist before automatic enablement

`NativeSlotAuthority` currently denies every request. `StandingSlotAuthority`
implements the policy and finite lifecycle protocol but has no installed native
backend. Its backend must be an independent scheduler/owner process, inaccessible
for writes from the Executive/LLM. A same-user Python callback or private JSON
flag is not isolation. No native standing-policy issuer is supplied or enabled.

Implement/qualify the backend against these exact methods:

- `exclusive(commission_id, seconds)`: nonblocking same operational runtime.lock;
  refuse normal stationary recognition even if its latest frame is momentarily
  quiet. Inventory every camera, Hailo, telemetry/watchdog, serial, physical task,
  gameplay and outstanding lease owner. Unknown owner/deadline means refusal.
- `inspect()`: trusted current snapshot with all fields defined in
  `StandingSlotAuthority._check`. Reservation must cover startup, prewarm, <=30s
  request, unload and independently forced service cutoff; <=90s default.
- `reserve_budget(commission_id, max_daily, cooldown)`: durable atomic unique
  commission/slot ledger; <=4 attempts/day, >=3600s cooling, survives reboot and
  concurrent clients. Policy pins qwen3:1.7b digest, reviewed revision and owner.
- `prepare(model, digest, ends_at)`: finite dedicated service launch and prewarm
  only after exclusion. Verify `/api/ps` and existing Admission receipt/boot/code/
  invocation binding; issue expiring admission in a separate protected authority
  domain. Never accept Executive-generated safe-idle assertions.
- `finish(ends_at)`: revoke on primary arrival, cancel client, wait for observed
  unload/empty cgroup or independently qualified forced cutoff before releasing
  exclusion. Death of the Executive must not disable cutoff or release safety
  reservation prematurely. Unknown completion marker survives all restarts.

The external authority must compare disabled/revoked policy on every check and
have an independently controlled instant revoke switch. `StandingPolicy()` is
disabled by default. The Executive queue has an additional global durable latch
on any possible outstanding completion; it cannot erase or clear that latch.
Operator clearance must preserve evidence, prove old service quiescent and use a
new reviewed decision notebook/attestation; never delete a marker to retry.
