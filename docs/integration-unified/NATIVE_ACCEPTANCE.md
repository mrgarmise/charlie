# Staged native acceptance and rollback

This checkpoint is not deployable or physically qualified. Do not operate actuators with it until the outstanding unified authority and independent device-specific qualification are implemented and reviewed. Firmware flashing is outside this procedure.

On the Pi, preserve every current checkout, firmware image, calibration profile, and raw record. In a new disposable directory, fetch the integration branch and verify the intended commit, parents and tree (`git show -s --format='%H %P %T' HEAD`; compare with the PR). Record OS, Python, source tree, HEF, installed firmware and calibration hashes; identify unknowns as unknown. Run the device-free focused suite and full suite before hardware access. Record outputs and preserve failures. Do not change existing checkouts or grant files.

Further stages require a separately reviewed revision and actual installed source and identities: (1) one producer and multiple independent camera subscribers, source-switch and fault checks; (2) existing pico object labels and disarmed RP2040 status/display; (3) trace and witnessed face tracker acquisition, response, loss, STOP, pan and handoff parity; (4) inspect installed ELEGOO MCU/ESP32 source and verify the sensor query is read-only before issuing it; establish units, clock, freshness, geometry, FOV and faults; (5) independent supervised STOP, device-local expiry, disconnect, braking, obstacle, floor, zone, wiring and actuator calibration qualification; (6) independently qualified proxy trial followed only then by willing-cat follow through normal Executive, including interruption, decline and revocation. A sent command or ACK does not prove physical motion or STOP. An ultrasonic return does not establish pet range or swept-corridor clearance.

Stop on uncertain delivery, stale or invalid sensor data, unexpected motion, animal distress, lost supervision or failed isolation. Use the independent physical cutoff. Preserve logs without alteration. Do not restart until the failure is understood and independently requalified. To roll back, stop the isolated integration process, disconnect or cut actuator power as appropriate, and resume the previously validated checkout using its existing procedure. Do not reset, overwrite, or delete that checkout or its evidence. Record the actual source and hardware state after rollback.
