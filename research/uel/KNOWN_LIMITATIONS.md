# Known limitations

- The profile is a Python simulation contract. Trusted external JSON registration, slew and group limits, energy and thermal protection contracts are pending.
- The inquiry helper does not implement the full question state machine, archive retrieval, provenance graph, or independent validation of caller-supplied observation references.
- SQLite replay currently supports the research schema. No sourced physical pan/tilt recordings were integrated or used for performance claims.
- The static model is local to observed dynamics. Prediction intervals undercovered in synthetic tests. The planner has yet to demonstrate fewer trials to a fixed target.
- Auditable qualification and simulation skill export, migrated evidence schemas, robust cross-process session ownership, and formal target resource measurements are pending.
- Optional local LLM integration remains gated on full A–D acceptance.
