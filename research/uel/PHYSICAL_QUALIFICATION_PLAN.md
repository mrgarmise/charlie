# Future pan/tilt commissioning

Requires separate operator approval and a compatible, independently qualified runtime. Start with an unpowered inspection. Confirm actual GP4/GP5/GP10 assignments, mechanical clearance in both axes, neutral and physical end-stops, servo supply separation and common grounding against the actual installation. Document current limiting and brownout behavior with an independently testable power cutoff. Establish exclusive executor ownership. Verify an accessible E-stop and device-local watchdog with motor power isolated before relying on either.

Under future authorization, capture a stationary camera baseline and supervise one short, single-axis move comfortably inside the calibrated range. Set the signed lease's axis, rate, range and duration before energizing. A human must be in reach of the physical cutoff. Monitor visual tracking, supply current and interruptions. Cancel on telemetry loss, abnormal current, binding or unexpected direction. Qualify each direction and fault response independently. On a failed check remove servo power, restore the approved stationary configuration and preserve logs. Never use a learner or local language model as the cutoff or watchdog.
